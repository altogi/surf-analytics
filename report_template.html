Wave cards

Every card uses the same scale (map and time), so a longer ride really looks longer. Colours follow the speed scale above; the grey stub is the last 8 s of paddle-in. Click a card to open the wave in the map.

How these are measured
  • Attempted: every burst of speed toward the shore. That's a caught wave, a short glide (no strokes but too slow or short to count), or a sprint where you never stopped paddling.
  • Caught: a stroke-free stretch reaching 1.6 m/s or more, lasting at least 5 s and covering 8 m or more toward shore. Stroke-free means you stood up.
  • Waiting: time sitting in the lineup, meaning under 0.3 m/s average over 30 s with no strokes.
  • Paddled: distance covered while paddling, including paddling back out after rides.
  • Max speed: the highest smoothed speed on a caught wave. Open Water mode filters GPS for swimming speeds, so this understates real board speed. It's consistent between sessions, though.
  • Wave style looks at the speed through the ride, smoothed over 5 s: quick dive = peak in the first 40% of the ride and the last third at least 10% (of peak) slower than the first; late pop-up = peak in the last 40% and the last third at least 10% faster; otherwise balanced.
